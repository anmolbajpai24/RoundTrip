Everything the group keeps asking about, in one place

Itinerary

A day-by-day plan with places, legs and notes — plus the map and the weather for each day, so "what's tomorrow?" answers itself.

Packing

Your own packing list, private to you, that survives the night-before chaos. Tick things off as they hit the suitcase.

Outfits

Plan what you're wearing for each day of the trip, with photos — and keep it to yourself or share it with the group.

Budget

Log expenses as they happen and see where the money went — shared with the trip so nobody's doing spreadsheet archaeology later.

Bookings

A checklist of what's booked and what isn't, with the tickets and confirmations uploaded right where everyone can find them.

Together, live

Invite your trip-mates with a short join code. Everyone edits the same trip and changes sync instantly across phones.

Feels like an app, works anywhere

Roundtrip runs in the browser and installs to your home screen on iPhone and Android — no app store. It keeps working with no signal and syncs back up when you're online, which is exactly what you want halfway up a mountain.
