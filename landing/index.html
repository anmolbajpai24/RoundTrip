How it works

  1. 1

    Make the trip

    Add the dates and stops. Already have the itinerary you send on WhatsApp? Paste it in and Roundtrip sorts it into days.

  2. 2

    Share the link

    Post it in the group. People tap it, type their name and they're in. No app store, no sign-up, no code to copy.

  3. 3

    Run it again

    Same route next weekend? Copy the trip to new dates in one go, with the plan and packing list already done.

The questions you answer every trip, answered once

Where's the pickup?

Pickup point, timings and the plan for each day, with a map and the weather. "What time tomorrow?" answers itself.

What do I bring?

Set the packing list once. Every traveller gets their own copy to tick off, so nobody turns up at Tosh without a jacket.

Who changed the plan?

Only you. Travellers see the trip but can't edit it, unless you switch editing on for the group.

Is everything booked?

A checklist of bookings, with tickets and confirmations attached where everyone can find them.

No signal?

The trip is saved on every phone. It works on the bus and up the hill, and syncs when the signal comes back.

Another app to install?

No. It opens in the browser from the link, and can sit on the home screen like an app on iPhone and Android.
